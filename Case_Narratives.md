# Case Narratives

## PV-101 — Nausea
A 34-year-old female from India experienced nausea 3 hours after use of Velunex. The outcome was reported as recovered and the event was recorded as not serious. No additional concomitant-treatment information was reported.

## PV-102 — Loss of Consciousness
A 61-year-old male from India experienced loss of consciousness 2 days after use of Velunex. The outcome was reported as recovering. Emergency hospital admission was recorded in the seriousness field. Concomitant information was unknown.

## PV-103 — Rash
A 27-year-old female from the United Kingdom experienced a mild rash 4 days after use of Velunex. The outcome was reported as recovered and the event was recorded as not serious. No rechallenge was reported.

## PV-104 — Appendicitis
A patient from India, with age not reported and sex recorded as unknown, experienced appendicitis 3 weeks after use of Velunex. The outcome was reported as recovered. Hospitalisation was recorded in the seriousness field. Medical history was incomplete.

## Narrative quality principles applied
- Chronological order is based only on the supplied relative time-to-onset.
- No exact calendar dates were created.
- No causality conclusion was added.
- No treatment, dose, route, indication, investigation, or laboratory result was invented.
- Source uncertainty and missing information were retained.
